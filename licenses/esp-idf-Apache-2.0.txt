ESP-IDF and Arduino-ESP32
Used by Pi-MFX: build toolchain and runtime for the optional ESP32-S3 foot
controller firmware. Pi-MFX ships firmware source only; the framework itself is
supplied by the user's PlatformIO or ESP-IDF installation.
Upstream: https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf
          https://github.com/espressif/arduino-esp32
