HOW Pi-MFX USES IT

Pi-MFX links dynamically against the unmodified libasound provided by the
operating system. No ALSA source is copied into or distributed with Pi-MFX, and
no ALSA source is modified. Users are free to replace their system libasound
with their own build; the LGPL relinking freedom is preserved because the
linkage is dynamic.
